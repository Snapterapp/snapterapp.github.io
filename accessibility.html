Accessibility

What helps in Snapter if you see, hear or move differently, and where to turn it on.

It follows your iPhone

  • Light or dark: Snapter follows your phone’s appearance. To choose for yourself: Settings → Theme.
  • Reduce Motion: if it’s on in your iPhone’s accessibility settings, Snapter tones down its moving effects. In the app: Settings → Appearance & accessibility → Reduce motion.
  • Increase Contrast: if it’s on on your iPhone, Snapter makes the text stronger and the backgrounds plainer. In the app: Settings → Appearance & accessibility → High contrast.

More in the app

  • Settings → Appearance & accessibility → Larger text makes the text and buttons a little bigger. Snapter doesn’t follow the text size you set for your whole iPhone yet; we’re working on it.
  • Settings → Appearance & accessibility → Colour-blind friendly colours swaps the greens, reds and oranges for colours that are easier to tell apart, and underlines the timer when time is running out.
  • Settings → More sound options → Vibration only, no sound: the game stays silent and vibrates instead, a little more strongly.

Something hard to use?

Tell us: Settings → Report a problem, or email snapterapp@gmail.com.
