Privacy policy

Last updated: 19 November 2026

Your account

You don't need an account. Your nickname and avatar live on your phone and in the game room while you play. If you choose to sign in, your progress and Host Pass are kept with your account, and your email is kept encrypted, only to send you account emails (sign-in links, security notices, and any you turn on), plus a fingerprint of it to find your account.

Your photos

Photos you take are sent to our server and to the AI service that judges them (Google Gemini). A photo starts sending as soon as you take it, so the judge can answer sooner; if you retake it, that copy is deleted at once and never kept. During a round's reveal, the players in your room see everyone's last photo of that round, but only a photo that has passed every safety check: one that hasn't is never shown to anyone else. Every photo is deleted within 24 hours. Before a photo is sent, the app also checks it on your phone, and a photo that looks sexual isn’t sent at all. That check runs entirely on the phone; all it tells our server is whether it could run. When voice clips are switched on, the winner of a round in a private room can hold a button to record a clip of up to 3 seconds for the room: it’s sent to our server, only the players in that room hear it, and it’s deleted with the room (within 24 hours at most). The microphone is used only while you hold the button.

When rooms are deleted

Rooms are deleted within 10 minutes of the last player leaving. Photos are deleted within 24 hours, with two exceptions: a photo you send with a report about the judge, or one a challenge proved wrong, is kept up to 90 days to improve the judge; a photo that led to a strike or ban is kept for moderators until 30 days after it ends (for a permanent ban, as long as it stands).

People, ads and stats

Only take photos of things, not of people who haven't agreed to it. We use no advertising and no third-party trackers. We count anonymous totals, such as games played, to keep the game running well.

On this phone

Your settings, levels and sticker album are kept on this device. Deleting the app or clearing its data removes them. The app also makes a random ID for this phone the first time it opens (not Apple's advertising ID), so your progress, Daily results and age answer stay together without an account.

Game records

We keep gameplay stats (the nicknames you used, games, photo results, reports and strikes) to run the game and for moderation; only the Snapter team sees them. When you report a photo, we keep the photo, the reason you picked, who took it, who reported it and what our checks said until a person has decided; nobody you report is told who reported them, and you aren't told what happened to them. Delete my data, on the Terms page, removes them. When you write to us through Report a problem or by email, we keep what you send (and the game, if you attach it) to answer you and fix the problem. If the app crashes, it sends us the error and where in the app it happened, with no name or ID, so we can fix it. Your player ID (and your friend code, if you share it) lets friends add you and keeps reports and bans tied to the right player. Questions or privacy requests: snapterapp@gmail.com

The Snap Pass

The Snap Pass (seasons, quests, coins and cosmetics) is kept with your account or this phone, with your time zone so daily quests change at your midnight. What you wear is seen by the players in your rooms. Delete my data removes it all. When you buy the Host Pass, Snap Pass Premium, coins or an item, Apple sends us a record of the purchase (its transaction number, the product and, for the Host Pass, whether it's in a trial, renewing, cancelled or refunded); we keep it with your account or phone so what you bought works on every device. The Host Pass and Snap Pass Premium are separate purchases, each recorded on its own. We never see your card. A host's own packs (their names and items) are kept the same way, so they can use them again.

Who else handles it

Who handles your data for us: our hosting provider (Fly.io, where the game server and its data live); Google, whose Gemini AI judges your photos (and, only if we turn it on as a backup, Anthropic's Claude); Resend, which delivers our emails and so gets your address when it sends one; and Apple, which takes payments. Your IP address is kept up to 30 days to stop abuse, and with a ban while it stands. Nothing is sold, and nobody uses your data for advertising. We never ask for your precise location (no GPS). We do keep your country and time zone: the country comes from your phone's region setting or is worked out from your IP address, and is used for the flag next to your Daily results, the age rule and our own player statistics; the time zone makes daily quests change at your midnight.

Children and age

Snapter’s games are for players aged 13 and over. The first time you open the app, it asks your birth month and year, on a screen that doesn’t suggest any answer, and works out your age from them. On iPhones that support it, the app also asks Apple’s Declared Age Range for your age range, and only notes whether it agrees. Your birth month and year are sent to our server with a random ID for your phone, your language and your country, so the 13-and-over rule can be applied; they’re stored encrypted, only Snapter’s admins (signed in with a password and a one-time code) can see the age worked out from them, and other players never see them. If you entered them wrong, Settings → Change my age corrects them, once every 7 days, and we record the date of each change. Under 13, you can open the app and look around, but you can’t play: no account, email, friends, purchases or photos. For under-13 players we keep only the random phone ID, the age (and the encrypted birth month and year it comes from), the date, the language, the country and that play is blocked, and delete it all after 30 days without the app being opened. For everyone else it’s kept with the rest of your player data, and Delete my data removes it.

Delete my data

Removes your game records, admin notes about you, daily challenge results, crew memberships, invites, strikes and any photo kept for a strike or ban, and everything stored on this phone. Bans and safety incident records stay, as the law allows.

In the app: Settings → Terms, privacy and safety → Delete my data. Or email snapterapp@gmail.com from anywhere and we'll do it for you.
